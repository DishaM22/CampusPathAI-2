# How to rate (about 15 minutes)

You will judge whether an event is a good suggestion for a student. Each row of the sheet is one student and one event.

1. Open **your own copy** of the sheet in Excel (`relevance_rater_A.csv` or `relevance_rater_B.csv`).
2. Fill the **relevance** column on every row with one number:

| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **0** | Not relevant. This student would have no reason to go. |
| **1** | Somewhat relevant. It fits partly (a related skill or interest, or a useful general event). |
| **2** | Highly relevant. It clearly fits this student's skills, interests or career goal. |

3. Judge only from what the row shows (the student's year, interests, skills, goal, domains, and the event's title, category, skills and description). Think: "If I were advising this student, would I tell them to attend this?"
4. **Work alone.** Do not discuss ratings with the other rater until both sheets are saved. Do not look at any recommendation lists.
5. Leave no row blank. Use only 0, 1 or 2.
6. Save as **CSV (Comma delimited)**, keeping the same file name and the `pair_id` column unchanged.

Students are shown as S1, S2, ... and contain no names or contact details. Please do not try to find out who they are, and do not share the sheet outside the project.
